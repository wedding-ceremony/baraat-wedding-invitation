BARAAT DIGITAL WEDDING INVITATION — PEARL & GOLD
=================================================
Mobile-first interactive invitation for cPanel. No database required.

PREVIEW ON COMPUTER
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open index.html in Chrome, Safari or Edge.
3. Tap "TAP TO OPEN".
4. Scratch the metallic gold foil with mouse or touch.
5. After 60% is scratched, the remaining foil clears automatically.
6. Tap Continue, flip the invitation card, then view venue and contacts.

PREVIEW ON PHONE
Upload the contents of the baraat-invitation folder to a temporary folder on your cPanel, for example:
public_html/wedding-preview/
Then open:
https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com/wedding-preview/
Use Safari on iPhone or Chrome on Android.

CPANEL UPLOAD
1. cPanel > File Manager > public_html (or your chosen subfolder).
2. Upload this ZIP and choose Extract.
3. Ensure index.html is directly inside the target folder.
4. Open the URL on your phone.

FEATURES
- Pearl & champagne-gold frame artwork supplied by the customer.
- Tap-to-open first screen.
- Working touch/mouse scratch-to-reveal foil.
- 60% scratch threshold clears remaining foil.
- Date reveal: 11 December 2026.
- Invitation card with 3D flip.
- Venue and Google Maps search link.
- Countdown to 11 Dec 2026, 6:00 PM Pakistan time.
- Individual WhatsApp and Call actions for all three contacts.
- Replay invitation and replay scratch.
- Responsive layout, iOS safe-area and reduced-motion support.
- noindex/nofollow meta directives.

IMPORTANT
- The supplied pearl-and-gold frame image is used as the opening artwork.
- The floral reference image was not used because the written brief says no floral decorations.
- Music is not included in this build; this avoids forced audio and respects iOS Safari's user-gesture rules. A licensed instrumental track can be added later if requested.
- Google Maps opens a search for the supplied venue address. Confirm the exact venue pin before sharing widely.
- noindex/nofollow discourages indexing; it does not password-protect the page.
- Google Fonts are loaded online; system fallbacks are included.
